About Me

I am a multidisciplinary artist and designer based in Aachen, Germany, with a passion for blending traditional craftsmanship with contemporary design.

With a Master's degree in Fashion Design from Accademia di Belle Arti di Roma and a Bachelor's in Jewelry Design from Shandong University of Art and Design, I bring a unique cross-disciplinary perspective to my work. My practice spans fashion illustration, jewelry design, oil painting, photography, leather crafting, and AI-generated art.

I have professional experience as a Designer Assistant at Valentino, and I founded and managed my own art and culture studio for over three years. I am proficient in four languages—Chinese, English, Italian, and German—and work fluently across digital and traditional media.
